K. David Černy. 2014, kinetic sculpture in a public space, stainless steel, electronics.
The height of the mobile sculpture is 10.6 metres. It consists of 42 rotating stainless steel panels, which rotate independent of each other. Its total weight is 39 tons, whereas the weight of the stainless steel sheeting is 24 tons. It contains 21 motorised modules and 1 kilometre of cables.
